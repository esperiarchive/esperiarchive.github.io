# Help improve the archive

[English](CONTRIBUTING.md) · [한국어](docs/CONTRIBUTING.ko.md) · [日本語](docs/CONTRIBUTING.ja.md) · [简体中文](docs/CONTRIBUTING.zh-CN.md)

Found a missing line, a wrong portrait, or something that differs from the game? A screenshot, a recording, or a short note can help. **One item is enough, in whichever language you prefer.**

## Send what you have

Use **Send feedback** on the [reader's home page](https://esperiarchive.github.io/), or [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/esperiarchive/esperiarchive.github.io/issues). The feedback form follows the language selected in the reader.

Tell us the story or character name and what you noticed. Add a reader link, screenshot, or recording if you have one. For a long video, a timestamp helps.

**Existing recordings and partial reports are welcome.** You can help without replaying a quest or checking the whole list.

## What needs checking?

The [current requests](docs/CURRENT_CHECKS.md) cover:

- **Dialogue order:** when a line plays relative to an objective or another line.
- **Official wording:** missing game text, including passages shown with a machine translation.
- **NPC conversation locations:** lines already included in the reader, including the Gleamtails' squeaks. Existing recordings can help confirm where they play.

Each entry gives the story, objective where known, and the lines to look for. Pick anything you recognize.

**Objective:** Find Talene  
**Lines to check:**

> It's as cool as ever nearby.

> No sign of Talene. Maybe she's somewhere else?

If you remember this part, tell us which line came first and which objective was on screen. A short description is useful even without a recording.

## Other corrections

For text corrections, say whether the wording comes from the game or is your own translation. For display problems, mention your device and reading mode if you can.

Documentation and code contributions are welcome too. You can report any archive problem here without finding a different repository.
